KOKIs (Non Sequentials) are knockin options that can knockout at any time.  These options differ from the sequential KIKO options in that they do not require the option to be knocked in prior to being knocked out.  A KOKI can knock out at any time before or after the KI trigger is reached: If the Knock-In is reached first, it can still be knocked out, and if the Knock-Out is reached first, it can no longer knock in.
