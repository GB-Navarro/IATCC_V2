A Knock-In Knock-Out Forward is a hedging strategy that involves the purchase of a Knock-Out option and the sale of a KIKO option.  The holder takes advantage of favorable market evolutions until the Knock-In barrier is reached.

If neither the Knock-In nor the Knock-Out have been reached, there are two cases: 1) If the Knock-Out option is in-the-money, the holder is hedged at the strike.  2) If the Knock-Out option is out-of-the-money, the holder can be hedged at the prevailing spot rate.

If the Knock-In barrier is reached first, the holder is hedged at the strike level as long as the Knock-Out barrier is not reached.  If the Knock-Out barrier is reached thereafter, the holder is loses his hedging position.  If the Knock-Out barrier is reached first, the holder loses his heding position, no matter whether the Knock-In is reached thereafter or not.

The strategy can be made zero cost by using the OVML 'Solve For' functionality.
